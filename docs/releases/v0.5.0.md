# StockOrNot v0.5.0 — Ticker intelligence

StockOrNot has been redesigned around a per-ticker intelligence dashboard that keeps professional news tone and community opinion separate.

## Highlights

- Real ticker profiles and recent news from Massive
- Transparent, recency-weighted news sentiment across 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day windows
- Hot and Not voting directly inside every ticker dashboard
- Crowd-versus-news divergence revealed after voting
- Database-backed ticker search, random discovery, and leaderboards
- D1 caching with graceful stale-data fallback

## Signal methodology

News articles use Massive's ticker-specific positive, neutral, or negative insight. StockOrNot removes duplicate normalized titles, allows at most four articles per publisher, and applies exponential recency weighting. At least two eligible articles are required for a score.

Crowd sentiment is normalized to the same −100 to +100 range. Divergence is Crowd minus News. These signals describe sentiment and coverage tone, not expected returns or investment quality.
