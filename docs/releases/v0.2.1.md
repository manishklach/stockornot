# StockOrNot 0.2.1 — Focused voting

This release sharpens StockOrNot around the core Hot-or-Not experience.

## Highlights

- The main card now focuses on the ticker and full company or fund name.
- Market charts, quotes, and provider metadata are removed from the visible MVP.
- Ticker pages now pair a clean identity card with the crowd verdict.
- Voting, rankings, filtering, search, and persistent sentiment remain unchanged.

The result is faster, simpler, and closer to the playful original product concept.
