# StockOrNot 0.2.0 — Real market charts

StockOrNot now replaces its illustrative chart fixtures with provider-backed adjusted daily price history.

## Highlights

- Explore accurate 5D, YTD, and 1Y adjusted price charts for every curated stock and ETF.
- See the latest close, selected-period return, high/low range, volume, and most recent market date.
- Keep the Massive credential server-side while caching normalized responses in Cloudflare D1 for 15 minutes.
- Fall back to the last cached result when the provider is temporarily unavailable.

## Data notes

The chart uses adjusted daily aggregate bars and may be delayed. It is not a real-time quote or investment advice. Public production use should proceed only after confirming the applicable market-data licensing and redistribution terms.
